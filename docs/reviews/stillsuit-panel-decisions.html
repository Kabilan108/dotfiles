What the choices mean in practice

The parts most likely to be misread during implementation.

Unread is a view state

Opening the notification center marks only the rows present at that moment as read. A notification that arrives while the center is open still increments the count. Dismissal and read state remain separate.

Wi-Fi has one native common path

Everyday personal Wi-Fi stays inside Stillsuit. Enterprise and hidden networks open NetworkManager's editor because their credential and protocol branches do not belong in the first native version.

Battery alerts do not depend on the shell

A user service owns thresholds, dedupe, and hysteresis. Stillsuit displays its notifications and battery state, but a shell reload cannot suppress the watcher.

Agent warm reopen is deployment work

The source optimization stays inactive until a later user-run rebuild and planned session transition. Removing the bar item does not change the Mod+` workflow.

Recording and meeting history

Operational recovery in Stillsuit. Long-term access in Obsidian.

CaptureChoose output, title, desktop audio, and microphone.

ProcessQueued and running stages remain durable outside the shell.
RecoverFailed rows show the error, details, and a manual Retry action.
ArchiveCompleted minutes open in Obsidian, the long-term record.

The recent list is a work queue, not an archive

The panel shows no more than the chosen row cap. Queued, processing, and failed meetings rank ahead of completed ones. Completed meetings fill any remaining slots, with the newest first.

If unfinished work exceeds the cap, the visible list shows the newest actionable rows and an overflow count. Older actionable rows move into the first page as shown jobs complete. Obsidian remains the durable destination for completed minutes.

Retry is always manual. A click disables the button while dispatch is pending, increments the attempt on the same job, and moves that row back to queued. The shell never retries a failed meeting on its own.
