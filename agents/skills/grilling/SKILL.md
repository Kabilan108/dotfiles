# Grilling

Read the available context first. Identify decisions that materially change the design and ask about those the repository cannot settle. Explain the tradeoff and give your recommendation.

Group independent questions into a manageable round. Hold dependent questions until their prerequisites are settled. Do not ask the user to research facts you can inspect. Stop when the important decisions are clear enough for the requested next step; record remaining assumptions instead of forcing an exhaustive interview. This mode does not itself authorize implementation or delegation.
