# Artifact checks

Run the lint and decide on each warning. Verify changed content and links. Render new or materially changed layouts where readability needs inspection: diagrams for crossed arrows and clipped labels, charts against their data, mockups for clipping. Test the controls you changed. Choose viewport checks for the likely reader rather than a fixed matrix.

Check a render where the reader will see it. For a PageBin page, that is the PageBin viewer, not only the local file: the viewer frames the artifact from its own origin and shows it over white. Look at a new PageBin page at about 390px wide, in light and dark when it follows the reader's setting: text clears the screen edge, and the page's own background and palette hold. Verify the published identity and content through PageBin when publishing. A text-only update to a previously checked layout does not require repeating every visual check.
